## Privacy Rules For Characters

- Use roles instead of real names unless explicit consent exists.
- Remove contact details, addresses, private usernames, emails, phone numbers,
  credentials, tokens, and personal identifiers.
- Third-party characters should be composited or anonymized by default.
- Company names and public entities must not be used to imply endorsement,
  employment, access, or affiliation.
