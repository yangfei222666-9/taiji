## Source Material

Allowed source material:

- Real dated notes.
- Real closeouts.
- Real Git status and PR gate evidence.
- Real TaijiOS evolution records.
- Real AI-assisted learning traces.
- Real four-book mappings.
- Real life systems notes.
- Real new-computer and workstation preparation records.
- Real blockers, mistakes, and recovery notes.

Blocked source material:

- Secrets, API keys, tokens, credentials, passwords.
- Emails, phone numbers, addresses, or private identifiers.
- Private third-party identities without consent.
- Fabricated achievements.
- Claims of employment or access not backed by evidence.
- AI-generated scenes presented as documentary truth.
