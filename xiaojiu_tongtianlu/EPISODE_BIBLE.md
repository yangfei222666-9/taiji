# Episode Bible

This file defines the first-season story structure for a future film or AI short
drama adaptation. It is a story bible, not a production-ready script.

## Series Premise

Xiaojiu Tongtianlu follows an ordinary person who believes the world hides deep
systems, then starts using AI, TaijiOS, four-book doctrine, life systems
thinking, and engineering discipline to move toward SpaceX-level engineering
capability.

Core tension:

```text
The dream is huge.
The evidence is small.
The only honest path is weekly closeout.
```

## Three-Layer Story Rule

| Layer | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Evidence layer | Real events, artifacts, dates, git gates, closeouts |
| Mythic layer | Journey, Yijing, Shanhai, Yongle, life systems symbolism |
| Cinematic layer | Visual compression and drama, always labeled as dramatized |

The story may be cinematic, but the archive must remain honest.

## Season 01: Fangcunshan Opening

Theme:

```text
The ordinary person receives the workstation, defines the doctrine, and learns
that the first enemy is fake pass.
```

### Episode 1: Hidden Systems

Xiaojiu senses that ancient civilizations, strange myths, AI, and engineering
may all point toward hidden structures behind the world.

Evidence requirement:

- Early notes or conversations about ancient civilization and hidden systems.

What cannot be claimed:

- No scientific proof of hidden systems.

### Episode 2: SpaceX Dream

The mission becomes explicit: the long road toward SpaceX-level engineering.

Evidence requirement:

- Dated mission notes or closeout.

What cannot be claimed:

- No SpaceX readiness, employment, interview, or endorsement.

### Episode 3: Bodhi Protocol

The assistant becomes a Bodhi-style training protocol. The goal shifts from
inspiration to discipline.

Evidence requirement:

- Four-book or Journey mapping notes.

### Episode 4: Great Immortal Method / Learning Evolution

The long-term learning loop is defined:

```text
dream -> evidence -> closeout -> correction -> next gate
```

Evidence requirement:

- TaijiOS doctrine or closeout artifacts.

### Episode 5: Fangcunshan Workstation

The new computer becomes the Fangcunshan workstation: a training field, not an
instant capability upgrade.

Evidence requirement:

- New-computer runbook or setup closeout.

### Episode 6: Git Five Elements Mountain

Dirty repo, upstream-gone branches, PR gates, and untracked docs become the Five
Elements Mountain: the hard boundary that prevents fake pass.

Evidence requirement:

- Real git status, PR, or closeout evidence.

### Episode 7: Three AI Tools Formula

Codex, DeepSeek, Gemini, and future tools become the Somersault Cloud. TaijiOS
remains the audit layer.

Evidence requirement:

- AI routing notes and task closeouts.

### Episode 8: Four Books System

Journey to the West, I Ching, Shan Hai Jing, and Yongle Dadian become the
symbolic operating system.

Evidence requirement:

- Four-book mapping docs.

### Episode 9: Life Systems Closure

Biology completes the life layer: evolution, immune systems, nervous systems,
ecology, AI biology, biosecurity, and astrobiology.

Evidence requirement:

- Life systems protocol or mapping docs.

### Episode 10: Xiaojiu Tongtianlu

The fifth book is named. The protagonist decides to record the real evolution
before attempting any film or AI short drama.

Evidence requirement:

- Canon file and source index.

## Production Boundary

Season 01 is not ready for production until:

- Evidence sources are indexed.
- Privacy rules are enforced.
- Dramatized scenes are labeled.
- Character identities are sanitized.
- No fabricated achievements are present.
- No SpaceX readiness or employment claim appears.
