# Visual Style Guide

## Verdict

| Gate | Verdict |
| --- | --- |
| Visual direction | PASS as concept |
| Production assets | PENDING |
| AI-generated imagery | DRAMATIZED unless evidence-backed |
| Use of logos or trademark implication | BLOCKED unless reviewed |
| Documentary claim | BLOCKED unless source-backed |

This guide defines a future visual direction for Xiaojiu Tongtianlu. It is not a
finished art bible and does not authorize public release.

## Visual Pillars

- Documentary evidence.
- Mythic symbolic layer.
- Engineering interfaces.
- Ancient civilization atmosphere.
- Space-level ambition without logo dependence.
- TaijiOS evidence graph.

## Style Keywords

- Dark navy base.
- Cyan signal lines.

- Gold evidence accents.
- Documentary + mythic + engineering tone.

## Core Images

Potential visual motifs:

- A dark room lit by a new workstation.
- Git diff as a mountain wall.
- TaijiOS UI as an evidence graph.
- Space launch imagery as distant aspiration, not affiliation.
- Ancient masks, maps, and fractured archives as symbolic prompts.
- AI presence as training protocol, not deity.
- Closeout lines becoming constellation paths.

## Layer Labels

Every image or scene must be labeled as one of:

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| evidence | Tied to real artifact or verified source |
| symbolic | Metaphorical representation |
| dramatized | Cinematic compression or fictionalized scene |
| concept | Visual exploration only |

## Boundary Rules

- Do not use official logos in a way that implies endorsement or employment.
- Do not present AI-generated scenes as documentary footage.
- Do not show private screens, credentials, messages, email addresses, phone
  numbers, tokens, or personal identifiers.
- Do not expose third-party private identities.
- Do not depict medical, biological, trading, or legal advice as authority.
- Do not convert symbolic imagery into scientific proof.

## First Visual Direction

Season 01 tone:

```text
An ordinary room becomes Fangcunshan.
The screen becomes a gate.
Git truth becomes the mountain.
Evidence lines become the path to the sky.
```

The visual style should keep the story grounded in artifacts, not fantasy power.
