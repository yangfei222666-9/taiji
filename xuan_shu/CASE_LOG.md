# XuanShu Case Log

## Purpose

Record observations before outcomes so the research lane cannot keep only hits and hide misses.

Authority: `learning_only` and `observe_only`.

## Rules

- Record the case before the outcome is known.
- Keep non-secret context only.
- Preserve misses and ambiguous cases.
- Do not use cases for trading, medical, legal, safety-critical, or high-stakes decisions.
- Do not upgrade symbolic interpretation into scientific fact.
- Do not claim SpaceX readiness from a case.

## Case Template

```text
Date:

Source:

Method:

TaijiOS mapping:
Evidence:
Allowed authority:

Next review date:
Closeout status:
Closeout notes:
```

## Verdict Vocabulary

Allowed:

- `PASS as metaphor`
- `PENDING as evidence`
- `PARTIAL`
- `AMBIGUOUS`
- `NOT_VERIFIABLE`
- `MISS_WITH_EVIDENCE`
- `HIT_WITH_EVIDENCE`
- `BLOCKED for execution`

Forbidden:

- `scientific fact` without evidence
- `trade signal`
- `medical advice`
- `legal advice`
- `SpaceX readiness`
- `repo-level PASS`
