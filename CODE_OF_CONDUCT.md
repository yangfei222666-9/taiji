# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We aim to maintain a respectful, constructive, and inclusive project environment.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Use welcoming and professional language.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give and receive feedback constructively.
- Focus discussions on what is best for the project.
- Avoid personal attacks, harassment, or disruptive behavior.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, threats, or intimidation.
- Discriminatory language or conduct.
- Personal insults or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Any behavior that makes collaboration unsafe or hostile.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this code.

## Reporting

Report issues to the maintainers through the repository's private security or contact channel when available.

Reports should include:

- What happened.
- Where it happened.
- When it happened.
- Any relevant links or screenshots.

Maintainers should review reports promptly and handle them with appropriate confidentiality.
